Privacy Policy — LinaWildMyth Autopost

Last updated: August 2026

LinaWildMyth Autopost ("the App") is a private, single-owner automation tool used to publish content to the Instagram account @linawildmyth on behalf of its owner, Vasilis Baroutas, operating the LinaWildMyth Etsy shop. This App is not distributed publicly and is not intended for use by third parties.

1. Data We Access

The App connects to the Instagram Graph API using OAuth access tokens authorized directly by the account owner. It accesses only:

  • The connected Instagram Business Account's ID and basic profile information (username, account type)
  • Media publishing endpoints, to upload and publish photos/carousels prepared by the owner

The App does not access, collect, or store data belonging to any other Instagram user, follower, or third party. It does not read direct messages, comments, or any personal data of people interacting with the account.

2. How Data Is Used

Access tokens and account identifiers are used solely to publish pre-approved content (product photos and carousels for the LinaWildMyth handmade goods shop) to the connected Instagram account. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics purposes.

3. Data Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's private machine and are not transmitted to any third-party service other than Meta's own APIs and Cloudinary (used solely as temporary public image hosting required by the Instagram Graph API's media upload process).

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Access tokens are retained only as long as needed for active use and can be revoked at any time by the account owner via Instagram/Facebook account settings, or by contacting us at the email below. No user data is retained after token revocation.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy or to request data deletion, contact: autobarutas@gmail.com
